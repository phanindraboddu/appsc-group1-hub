ANCIENT ANDHRA HISTORY

Satavahanas

Origin • Expansion • Administration • Society • Economy • Religion • Literature

📖 Overview

The Satavahanas were one of the earliest major indigenous dynasties to establish a large political power in the Deccan. Their rule occupies an important place in the history of ancient Andhra and the wider Deccan region.

For APPSC Group 1, the Satavahanas are important from the perspectives of political history, administration, economy, trade, society, religion, art, architecture and literature.

⚡ Quick Revision

🏛️ Dynasty

Satavahana Dynasty

👑 Important Rulers

Simuka, Satakarni, Gautamiputra Satakarni and Yajna Sri Satakarni.

🌍 Region

Deccan and parts of present-day Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Maharashtra.

🪙 Economy

Agriculture, internal trade and overseas commerce were important.

1. Origin and Expansion

The Satavahanas emerged as an important political power in the Deccan after the decline of Mauryan authority.

Their political influence expanded across different parts of the Deccan at different stages of their history.

The exact origin and early history of the dynasty remain subjects of historical debate, making this an important area for examination-oriented study.

2. Administration

King

The king was the central authority and played an important role in administration and military affairs.

Administrative Divisions

The kingdom was administered through territorial and local administrative units.

Military

The Satavahanas maintained military power to control their extensive Deccan territories.

3. Society

Satavahana society reflected a combination of Brahmanical traditions and Buddhist influences.

Inscriptions and donations provide important information about rulers, merchants, artisans, women and religious communities.

4. Economy and Trade

🌾 Agriculture

Agriculture formed an important foundation of the economy.

🛍️ Internal Trade

Trade networks connected different parts of the Deccan.

🚢 Overseas Trade

The Deccan participated in maritime trade networks linking India with the wider world.

🪙 Coins

Coins provide important evidence for the economic and political history of the dynasty.

5. Religion

Buddhism received substantial patronage during the Satavahana period. At the same time, Brahmanical traditions also continued to flourish.

Religious institutions and monuments received donations from rulers, merchants and other sections of society.

6. Art and Architecture

The Satavahana period contributed significantly to the development of Buddhist art and architecture in the Deccan.

Stupas, monasteries, sculptures and inscriptions provide valuable evidence for understanding the cultural history of the period.

7. Literature

Prakrit played an important role in the literary and inscriptional culture of the Satavahana period.

The period is associated with important literary developments and provides evidence of the cultural interaction between different traditions.
