Pollution & Solid Waste Management

APPSC Group-I • Science, Technology & Environment

Core coverage

Air, water, soil and noise pollution; source-pathway-impact-control approach; waste hierarchy, segregation, collection, recycling, treatment and safe disposal.

Concepts to master

Definition • underlying scientific principle • terminology • process/mechanism • applications • institutions/policy • benefits and limitations • India and Andhra Pradesh relevance.

Prelims strategy

Prepare statement-based distinctions, cause-effect links, applications and institutional roles. Do not memorise a technology or treaty name without knowing what it actually does.

Mains framework

Concept → mechanism → applications/significance → India/AP context → challenges/risks → governance or technological response → way forward.

Practice questions

  1. Scientific concepts should be connected to real applications. True.
  2. Benefits and risks should both be addressed in a Mains answer. True.
  3. An unverified question should be called an APPSC PYQ. False.

Practice only — not official PYQs.

Mains practice

Explain the scientific basis of Pollution & Solid Waste Management and examine its applications, challenges and policy relevance for India.

Quick revision

What → how → 3 applications → 2 challenges → institution/policy → AP/India example.
