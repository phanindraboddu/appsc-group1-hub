Mensuration

APPSC Group-I Prelims Paper II • General Aptitude

What to master

Perimeter, area, surface area and volume of common plane and solid figures; unit conversion and composite figures.

Method

Concept → worked pattern → timed practice → error log → reattempt. Write the minimum necessary steps and eliminate options whenever possible.

Practice drill

Solve 10 basic questions untimed, then 10 mixed questions under time. Record every error as concept, calculation, interpretation or time-management error.

Exam strategy

Attempt high-confidence items first. Avoid spending disproportionate time on a single puzzle. Recheck units, signs, percentages and the exact question asked.

Quick revision

Create one small sheet containing formulas/rules, 3 common traps and 2 solved examples for Mensuration.
