Ikshvakus

2026 syllabus mapping: Mains Paper II → History & Culture of Andhra Pradesh → Ancient → Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Salankayanas, Pallavas and Vishnukundins; social-economic conditions, religion, Telugu, literature, art & architecture, Jainism and Buddhism.

Prelims High-Yield Core

Political centre

Vijayapuri, identified with Nagarjunakonda in the Krishna valley.

Rulers to remember

Vasisthiputra Chamtamula I → Mathariputra Virapurushadatta → Vasisthiputra Ehuvala Chamtamula → Rudrapurushadatta.

Culture

Nagarjunakonda is central for inscriptions, Buddhist establishments, sculpture and Brahmanical religious evidence.

Mains Study Framework

DimensionWhat to prepare
Political historyRise after Satavahana decline, rulers, Vijayapuri and regional position.
Society & economyDonors, merchants, settlements, agrarian support and inscriptional evidence.
ReligionBrahmanical patronage alongside flourishing Buddhist establishments; role of royal women and donors.
Art & architectureNagarjunakonda stupas, monasteries, sculptures, inscriptions and relationship with the Amaravati artistic tradition.
Historical significanceContinuity and transition in post-Satavahana Andhra; importance of archaeological and epigraphic evidence.
