Infrastructure in India

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Prelims + Mains

Core coverage

Economic and social infrastructure; transport, energy, logistics, telecom/digital and urban systems; public investment, PPPs, infrastructure finance, regulation, network effects and multiplier effects.

Prelims focus

Definitions, classifications, institutions, financing models, network effects and statement-based conceptual questions.

Mains framework

Need → present structure → bottlenecks → financing/governance → inclusion and sustainability → Andhra Pradesh linkage → way forward.

Practice

  1. Infrastructure can raise economy-wide productivity. Answer: True.
  2. PPPs involve public-private participation in infrastructure/service delivery under contractual arrangements. Answer: True.
  3. Current project figures should be verified from latest official sources. Answer: True.
