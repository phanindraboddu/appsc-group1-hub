Industry & Industrial Policy

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Why industrialisation matters

Industry can raise productivity, diversify the economy, generate non-farm employment, deepen technology and create linkages with agriculture and services. Manufacturing policy therefore interacts with infrastructure, logistics, skills, finance, trade and innovation.

2. Industrial-policy evolution

India's early post-Independence strategy gave the public sector and licensing a major role in industrial development. The 1991 reform phase substantially reduced industrial licensing, opened more space for private investment and competition, and integrated the economy more deeply with global markets.

3. MSMEs

MSMEs matter for employment, entrepreneurship, supply chains and regional dispersion. Common constraints include access to finance, technology, markets, skilled labour, compliance capability and delayed payments. Use the current statutory classification only after checking the latest official notification because thresholds can change.

4. Industrial location

  • Raw materials and energy

5. AP angle

For Andhra Pradesh, connect industrial development with its coastline/ports, logistics, industrial corridors, food processing, electronics/manufacturing clusters, MSMEs and regional balance. Current project/status claims require latest official verification.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. The 1991 industrial reforms are associated with: A) expansion of licensing in all industries B) liberalisation and greater competition C) abolition of private industry D) end of foreign trade
    Answer: B.
  2. Industrial clusters can create: A) agglomeration benefits B) monsoon rainfall C) constitutional amendments D) census data
    Answer: A.
  3. MSMEs are important partly because of their role in: A) employment and supply chains B) monetary policy only C) currency printing D) judicial appointments
    Answer: A.
  4. Logistics costs can affect industrial competitiveness. A) True B) False
    Answer: A.
  5. Industrial policy has no connection with skills or infrastructure. A) True B) False
    Answer: B.
