Make in India, Start-up, SEZs & Corridors

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Make in India

Make in India is a manufacturing/investment initiative launched in 2014. For APPSC, study it as part of the broader effort to improve manufacturing competitiveness, investment, innovation, infrastructure and ease of doing business rather than as an isolated scheme.

2. Start-up ecosystem

Start-up policy seeks to encourage innovation and scalable entrepreneurship through a combination of regulatory facilitation, funding ecosystem support, incubation, intellectual-property support and market opportunities. Distinguish a start-up policy framework from the broader MSME universe.

3. SEZs

Special Economic Zones are specially designated areas operating under a dedicated legal/policy framework intended to promote economic activity and exports. Key analytical issues include infrastructure, trade facilitation, investment, employment, land, fiscal costs and integration with the domestic economy.

4. Industrial corridors

Industrial corridors combine transport connectivity with industrial nodes, logistics and urban/infrastructure development. Their economic logic is to reduce logistics friction and create clusters and investment ecosystems.

5. AP lens

Andhra Pradesh's long coastline, ports and location make port-led development, logistics and industrial corridors especially relevant. For specific corridor nodes, investments or project status, use the latest official State/Union source.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. Make in India was launched in: A) 1991 B) 2005 C) 2014 D) 2020
    Answer: C.
  2. An industrial corridor primarily combines: A) connectivity and industrial nodes B) only agriculture C) only taxation D) judicial districts
    Answer: A.
  3. SEZs are associated strongly with: A) designated economic zones and trade/investment facilitation B) monetary policy C) census D) elections
    Answer: A.
  4. A start-up ecosystem commonly emphasises: A) innovation and entrepreneurship B) only land ceilings C) currency printing D) food procurement
    Answer: A.
  5. Ports can be important to AP industrial strategy because they improve: A) maritime logistics connectivity B) rainfall C) literacy automatically D) monetary transmission
    Answer: A.
