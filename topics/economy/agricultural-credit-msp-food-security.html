Agricultural Credit, MSP & Food Security

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Agricultural credit

Farm credit finances seasonal operations and investment. Institutional channels include commercial banks, cooperative institutions and regional rural banks, with NABARD playing an important development/refinance role in rural credit architecture.

2. MSP

Minimum Support Prices are announced by the Union Government for specified crops after considering recommendations of the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) and other factors. MSP announcement and actual procurement are distinct concepts; procurement is concentrated in particular crops/regions and operates through relevant agencies and State arrangements.

3. Food security architecture

  • Availability: adequate food supply.
  • Access: households can obtain food economically and physically.
  • Utilisation: nutrition, health, sanitation and food quality matter.
  • Stability: availability/access should persist over time.

Public procurement, buffer stocks and the public distribution system are important parts of India's food-management architecture.

4. High-yield distinctions

5. Practice MCQs

  1. CACP is associated with recommendations concerning: A) MSP B) repo rate C) GST rate D) exchange rate
    Answer: A.
  2. NABARD is primarily associated with: A) rural/agricultural development finance B) defence procurement C) election management D) telecom licensing
    Answer: A.
  3. Food security includes: A) availability only B) availability, access, utilisation and stability C) exports only D) buffer stock only
    Answer: B.
  4. MSP announcement guarantees procurement of every unit offered by every farmer. A) True B) False
    Answer: B.
  5. Buffer stocks are connected with: A) food management B) securities trading C) judicial review D) census
    Answer: A.
