Planning & NITI Aayog

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Why planning matters

Economic planning coordinates public priorities, investment and development objectives. India moved from the Planning Commission and Five-Year Plan framework toward a policy-and-coordination model centred on NITI Aayog.

2. Planning in India: quick timeline

StageExam point
Planning CommissionEstablished in 1950 by executive resolution; it was neither a constitutional nor statutory body.
Five-Year PlansUsed national medium-term plans with changing priorities such as agriculture, industry, self-reliance, poverty reduction and inclusive growth.
NITI AayogCreated in 2015 by Union Cabinet resolution as the Government of India's policy think tank.

3. Planning Commission vs NITI Aayog

Planning CommissionNITI Aayog
Associated with Five-Year Plans and plan-resource allocationPolicy think tank; does not perform the former plan-fund allocation role
Top-down planning was a recurring criticismEmphasises cooperative and competitive federalism and State participation
Deputy Chairman was a prominent executive positionPrime Minister is Chairperson; Vice-Chairperson and other members support the institution

4. NITI Aayog — high-yield points

  • Promotes cooperative federalism through structured involvement of States.
  • Acts as a policy knowledge and innovation platform and supports long-term strategic thinking.
  • The Governing Council brings together the Prime Minister and State/UT leadership as provided in its institutional framework.
  • It is not the Finance Commission and does not replace the constitutional Finance Commission mechanism.

5. Prelims traps

  • Planning Commission and NITI Aayog were created by executive action, not by a constitutional amendment.
  • NITI Aayog is not a constitutional body.
  • Finance Commission is constitutional under Article 280; do not confuse it with NITI Aayog.
  • Planning is broader than public expenditure: it includes priorities, coordination, monitoring and development strategy.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. NITI Aayog was established in: A) 1991 B) 2000 C) 2015 D) 2020
    Answer: C.
  2. Which is a constitutional body? A) NITI Aayog B) Planning Commission C) Finance Commission D) National Development Council
    Answer: C.
  3. NITI Aayog is most closely associated with: A) judicial review B) cooperative federalism and policy strategy C) currency issue D) election management
    Answer: B.
  4. The Planning Commission was established in: A) 1947 B) 1950 C) 1952 D) 1956
    Answer: B.
  5. NITI Aayog directly replaced which institution in the national planning architecture? A) RBI B) Finance Commission C) Planning Commission D) GST Council
    Answer: C.

7. Mains framework

Question: Explain the transition from the Planning Commission to NITI Aayog and assess its significance for cooperative federalism.

Structure: planning background → institutional transition → cooperative/competitive federalism → policy and knowledge role → limitations/challenges → State/AP perspective → balanced conclusion.

8. Quick revision

1950: Planning Commission. 2015: NITI Aayog. Remember: NITI = policy/strategy/federal cooperation; Finance Commission = constitutional fiscal devolution.

APPSC deep core

  • India's planning history moved from the Planning Commission/Five-Year Plan framework toward NITI Aayog, established in 2015 as a policy think tank/cooperative-federal platform.
  • Revise broad planning phases and objectives: growth, self-reliance, poverty reduction, modernisation, inclusion and sustainability—without assuming each Plan had identical priorities.
  • Distinguish NITI Aayog from the Finance Commission: their constitutional/statutory status and functions are different.
  • APPSC Mains should connect planning to Centre–State coordination, evidence-based policy, district/regional disparities and outcome monitoring.

Mains framework

Planning rationale → historical evolution → institutional change → cooperative federalism → evidence/monitoring → State-level development.
