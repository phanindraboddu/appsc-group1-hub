National Income

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Why this topic is crucial

National-income accounting measures production and income in an economy and is a frequent source of conceptual Prelims questions. Focus on domestic vs national, gross vs net, market prices vs basic/factor concepts, nominal vs real and per-capita measures.

2. Core aggregates

TermMeaning
GDPFinal production within domestic territory during a period
GVAOutput minus intermediate consumption
GNIGDP adjusted for net primary income from the rest of the world
NDPGDP minus consumption of fixed capital (depreciation)
Per-capita measureAggregate divided by population

3. Three approaches

  • Production/value-added method: sum value added, avoiding double counting.
  • Income method: sum incomes generated in production.
  • Expenditure method: sum final expenditure components; in the familiar macro identity GDP = C + I + G + (X − M).

Conceptually, properly measured approaches describe the same economic activity from different sides.

4. Nominal and real

Nominal aggregates use current prices; real aggregates use constant-price valuation to isolate volume changes. A GDP deflator-type measure relates nominal and real aggregates and reflects broad price change in domestically produced final output.

5. Prelims traps

  • Intermediate goods are not separately added when their value is already embodied in final output — avoid double counting.
  • Transfer payments are not payments for current production and are treated differently from factor incomes in production accounting.
  • Sale of an existing asset is not itself new production; services generated in the transaction may be current output.
  • Domestic and national concepts differ because of cross-border primary income flows.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. NDP equals: A) GDP + depreciation B) GDP − depreciation C) GDP + imports D) GDP − exports
    Answer: B.
  2. Which method directly sums value added? A) Production method B) Monetary method C) Balance-sheet method D) Census method
    Answer: A.
  3. Real GDP primarily removes the effect of: A) population B) price change C) exports D) taxation
    Answer: B.
  4. In GDP = C + I + G + (X−M), X−M denotes: A) fiscal deficit B) net exports C) depreciation D) saving
    Answer: B.
  5. Double counting is avoided by focusing on: A) all intermediate sales plus final sales B) value added/final output C) only imports D) only taxes
    Answer: B.

Practice MCQs — not APPSC PYQs.

7. Mains framework

Explain what national accounts measure → principal aggregates → measurement approaches → nominal/real distinction → limitations of GDP as welfare indicator → complement with distribution, employment, human development and environment.

8. Quick revision

Gross − depreciation = Net. Domestic + net primary income from abroad = National. Nominal = current prices; Real = constant-price volume measure.
