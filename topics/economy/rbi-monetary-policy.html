RBI & Monetary Policy

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated notes

Why this topic matters

This chapter connects core economic concepts with policy analysis. Prepare definitions for Prelims and mechanisms, consequences, trade-offs and reform options for Mains.

Core syllabus map

  • RBI functions and monetary-policy objectives
  • Repo rate, standing facilities, CRR and liquidity tools
  • Inflation targeting and Monetary Policy Committee
  • Transmission from policy rates to credit and demand
  • Growth-inflation and financial-stability trade-offs

Concepts you must know

  • RBI is India's central bank and monetary authority.
  • Repo-rate changes influence the economy through the monetary-transmission mechanism.
  • CRR affects banks' lendable resources; open-market operations alter system liquidity.

How to analyse in Mains

Use the chain problem/context → policy instrument → transmission mechanism → intended benefit → possible cost/trade-off → implementation issue → balanced way forward. Distinguish a policy objective from the instrument used to achieve it.

Prelims traps

  • Do not treat related terms as synonyms.
  • Separate institutions from the instruments they operate.
  • Watch the direction of cause and effect in statement questions.
  • For current numerical values, use the latest official release rather than memorising a static webpage value.
