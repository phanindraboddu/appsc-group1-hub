Agriculture, Irrigation & Land Reforms

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Agriculture in development

Agriculture supports food security, rural livelihoods, raw materials, demand and allied-sector activity. APPSC preparation should connect productivity with irrigation, land relations, technology, markets and risk.

2. Cropping and productivity

  • Productivity depends on seeds, soil health, water, inputs, mechanisation, extension, credit, markets and risk management.
  • Cropping pattern responds to agro-climatic conditions, prices, irrigation, technology and policy incentives.
  • Diversification toward horticulture, livestock, fisheries and other allied activities can broaden farm income but requires value chains and risk management.

3. Irrigation

TypeExam point
Major/medium systemsCan expand assured irrigation but involve capital, maintenance and water-allocation issues
Minor irrigationIncludes smaller surface/groundwater sources under applicable classifications
Micro-irrigationDrip/sprinkler methods improve application efficiency where suitable

Important Mains themes: water-use efficiency, groundwater sustainability, command-area management and crop-water alignment.

4. Land reforms

Post-Independence land reforms broadly included abolition of intermediaries, tenancy reforms, ceilings and redistribution, and consolidation/record-related measures. Outcomes varied across States. Secure land records and tenancy arrangements remain important for investment and access to institutional services.

5. AP angle

For Andhra Pradesh, link agriculture with major river systems and irrigation, coastal/delta agriculture, dryland regions, horticulture, aquaculture and climate risks. Use official State data for project-specific/current claims.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. Drip irrigation is primarily intended to improve: A) water application efficiency B) customs collection C) exchange rate D) bank capital
    Answer: A.
  2. Abolition of intermediaries is associated with: A) monetary policy B) land reforms C) foreign trade D) securities regulation
    Answer: B.
  3. Crop diversification can help: A) broaden income sources B) eliminate all farm risk C) end irrigation needs D) fix exchange rates
    Answer: A.
  4. Groundwater over-extraction is primarily a concern of: A) water sustainability B) tariff policy C) stock exchanges D) census
    Answer: A.
  5. Land reform outcomes were identical across all States. A) True B) False
    Answer: B.

Practice questions — not APPSC PYQs.

7. Mains framework

Role of agriculture → productivity constraints → irrigation efficiency → land institutions → diversification/value chains → sustainability → AP-specific examples → reforms.
