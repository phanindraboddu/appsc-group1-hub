Sustainable Development

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Meaning

Sustainable development integrates economic progress, social inclusion and environmental protection while safeguarding the ability of future generations to meet their needs.

2. Three dimensions

EconomicSocialEnvironmental
Productivity, jobs, resilient infrastructureEquity, health, education, inclusionResource efficiency, biodiversity, pollution and climate resilience

3. SDGs

The UN 2030 Agenda contains 17 Sustainable Development Goals. For Economy, connect poverty, hunger, health, education, gender equality, clean energy, decent work, industry/infrastructure, reduced inequalities, sustainable cities, responsible consumption, climate action and institutions.

4. Key economic concepts

  • Externality: cost or benefit imposed on others and not fully reflected in market prices.
  • Polluter Pays Principle: polluters should bear pollution-control/remediation costs under the applicable framework.
  • Green growth: growth strategies that improve resource efficiency and reduce environmental pressures.
  • Circular economy: reduce waste through reuse, repair, recycling and resource recovery.
  • Inter-generational equity: present development should account for future generations.

5. India/AP answer dimensions

Use renewable energy, water efficiency, climate-resilient agriculture, public transport, waste management, coastal resilience, disaster risk reduction and sustainable urbanisation as analytical dimensions. In AP-specific answers, coastal vulnerability, irrigation/water management, agriculture and urban/industrial development provide useful linkages.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. How many SDGs are in the UN 2030 Agenda? A) 8 B) 12 C) 17 D) 21
    Answer: C.
  2. Inter-generational equity concerns: A) only current taxpayers B) fairness between present and future generations C) exchange rates D) bank capital
    Answer: B.
  3. A negative externality is best illustrated by: A) pollution affecting third parties B) private saving C) a salary payment D) a bank deposit
    Answer: A.
  4. Circular economy emphasises: A) higher waste generation B) reuse and resource efficiency C) only imports D) only taxation
    Answer: B.
  5. Sustainable development combines: A) economic goals only B) environment only C) economic, social and environmental dimensions D) trade only
    Answer: C.

Practice MCQs — not APPSC PYQs.

7. Mains framework

Definition → growth-environment-social trade-offs → climate/resource constraints → instruments and technology → inclusion/just transition → AP-specific vulnerabilities/opportunities → measurable outcomes and conclusion.

8. Quick revision

Sustainable development = Economy + Society + Environment + future generations. Remember: 17 SDGs, 2030 Agenda.
