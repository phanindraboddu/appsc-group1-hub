Employment, Rural & Urban Development

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Employment concepts

Employment analysis links economic growth with livelihoods. Distinguish labour force, workforce, labour-force participation, worker-population ratio and unemployment rate. The denominator used matters.

ConceptMeaning
Labour forcePersons employed plus persons unemployed but seeking/available for work under the statistical definition used
WorkforcePersons employed
LFPRLabour force as a proportion of the relevant population
Unemployment rateUnemployed as a proportion of labour force

2. Types of unemployment

  • Seasonal: work unavailable during parts of the year.
  • Disguised: more workers engaged than required; marginal productivity of some workers may be very low.
  • Structural: mismatch between economic structure, skills and available jobs.
  • Frictional: short-term unemployment during job search or transition.

3. Rural development

Rural development extends beyond agriculture to roads, irrigation, housing, sanitation, health, education, digital connectivity, non-farm employment, credit, self-help institutions and local governance. Mains answers should connect livelihood diversification with productivity and resilience.

4. Urban development

Urbanisation can raise productivity through agglomeration, but unmanaged growth creates congestion, housing shortages, pollution and pressure on water, transport and sanitation. Sustainable urban development therefore requires planning, municipal finance, affordable housing, mobility and basic services.

5. AP angle

Use Andhra Pradesh examples around rural livelihoods, agriculture-allied sectors, migration, urban growth, ports/industrial nodes and municipal infrastructure. Add current figures only from the latest official AP publications.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. Unemployment rate uses which denominator? A) Total population B) Labour force C) Workforce only D) Children only
    Answer: B.
  2. Disguised unemployment is commonly associated with: A) excess workers relative to work required B) inflation C) foreign trade D) taxation
    Answer: A.
  3. LFPR relates labour force to: A) relevant population B) GDP C) exports D) fiscal deficit
    Answer: A.
  4. Agglomeration economies are most closely linked with: A) urban concentration of activity B) monsoon failure C) currency issue D) customs duty
    Answer: A.
  5. Rural development is broader than agricultural production alone. A) True B) False
    Answer: A.
