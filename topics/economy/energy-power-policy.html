Energy & Power Policy

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Energy and development

Reliable, affordable and increasingly clean energy is fundamental to industry, agriculture, households and services. Energy policy must balance security, access, affordability, financial viability and environmental sustainability.

2. Energy sources

CategoryExamplesKey issue
FossilCoal, oil, natural gasEnergy security, imports, emissions
RenewableSolar, wind, small hydro, biomassVariability, storage, grid integration
NuclearNuclear fission-based powerLow operational carbon emissions, safety/waste/capital considerations
Large hydroReservoir/run-of-river projectsFlexible power potential plus ecological/social considerations

3. Power-sector chain

Generation → transmission → distribution → consumer. Distribution-company finances, technical/commercial losses, tariff design, metering, grid reliability and renewable integration are recurring policy themes.

4. Energy transition

  • Expand clean generation and transmission capacity.
  • Improve energy efficiency.
  • Develop storage and grid flexibility.
  • Electrify suitable end uses while maintaining reliability.
  • Manage transition impacts on workers, regions and consumers.

5. AP angle

APPSC answers can connect Andhra Pradesh with solar/wind potential, power needs of agriculture and industry, transmission/distribution, coastal energy infrastructure and the need for financially and environmentally sustainable supply. Verify current capacity/project figures from official sources before use.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. Which is renewable? A) Coal B) Solar C) Diesel D) Natural gas
    Answer: B.
  2. The electricity chain normally includes: A) generation, transmission and distribution B) only taxation C) only mining D) only banking
    Answer: A.
  3. Variable renewable integration can increase the importance of: A) storage and grid flexibility B) land ceilings C) customs only D) census
    Answer: A.
  4. Energy security concerns: A) reliable availability of energy at acceptable conditions B) only stock prices C) only rainfall D) only exports
    Answer: A.
  5. Energy efficiency can reduce energy required per unit of service/output. A) True B) False
    Answer: A.

Practice questions — not APPSC PYQs.

7. Mains framework

Energy-development link → security/access/affordability → power-sector structure → renewable transition → grid/storage/distribution reforms → just transition → AP-specific opportunities/challenges.
