Growth, Distribution & HDI

APPSC Group-I 2026 • Integrated Prelims + Mains preparation

1. Growth versus development

Economic growth is expansion of real output/income. Economic development is broader: growth together with structural transformation and improvements in capabilities and living conditions.

2. Distribution matters

Average income can rise while gains are distributed unevenly. Distribution analysis therefore examines who participates in growth and who receives its benefits. Important dimensions include income, wealth, region, gender, rural-urban location and access to public services.

Indicator/conceptUse
Per-capita incomeAverage income measure; does not reveal distribution
Lorenz curveGraphical representation of distribution
Gini coefficientSummary measure of inequality; higher values indicate greater inequality within the measure used
Multidimensional approachLooks beyond income to deprivations such as health and education

3. Human Development Index

UNDP's HDI combines three broad dimensions: long and healthy life, knowledge, and a decent standard of living. It is a composite development indicator rather than a complete measure of welfare.

DimensionCore indicator
HealthLife expectancy at birth
EducationExpected years of schooling and mean years of schooling
Standard of livingGNI per capita (PPP-based measure used by UNDP)

4. Why GDP alone is insufficient

  • GDP measures economic production, not distribution.
  • It does not directly capture health, education or many non-market aspects of welfare.
  • Environmental depletion and unpaid work require separate analytical treatment.
  • Therefore Mains answers should use GDP together with employment, distribution, human-development and sustainability indicators.

5. Andhra Pradesh lens

For AP answers, compare growth with employment, education, health, poverty, urban-rural outcomes and regional disparities. Use the latest official AP Socio-Economic Survey/Budget for current statistics rather than memorising stale figures.

6. Practice MCQs

  1. HDI includes which dimension? A) Military expenditure B) Health C) Stock-market capitalisation D) Gold reserves
    Answer: B.
  2. A Lorenz curve is primarily associated with: A) inflation B) income/wealth distribution C) exchange rates D) fiscal deficit
    Answer: B.
  3. Which statement is correct? A) Per-capita income fully describes inequality B) GDP and HDI are identical C) Development is broader than output growth D) HDI measures only income
    Answer: C.
  4. HDI's education dimension uses: A) literacy alone B) schooling indicators C) tax revenue D) unemployment alone
    Answer: B.
  5. A rise in average income necessarily means inequality fell. A) True B) False
    Answer: B.

Practice MCQs — not APPSC PYQs.

7. Mains framework

Question: “Economic growth is necessary but not sufficient for development.” Discuss.

Define growth/development → explain distribution and capabilities → HDI dimensions → employment and public services → sustainability → AP examples/current official data → conclude with inclusive human-centred growth.

8. Quick revision

Growth = more real output. Development = growth + structural/capability improvements. HDI = Health + Education + Standard of living.
