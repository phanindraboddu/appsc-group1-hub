APPSC deep core

  • RTI: transparency tool built around access to information held by public authorities, proactive disclosure, designated information officers, appeals and legally specified exemptions.
  • Public-service delivery laws: study the idea of notified services, time limits, designated officers, appeals/accountability; verify Andhra Pradesh-specific statutory/current arrangements before quoting details.
  • Social audit: community/public verification of programme records, expenditure, processes and outcomes; distinguish it from statutory financial audit and departmental inspection.
  • Transparency is most useful when linked with usable records, disclosure, grievance redress and corrective action.

Prelims traps

RTI vs grievance petition • proactive disclosure vs request-based disclosure • social audit vs CAG/statutory audit • transparency vs accountability.

Mains framework

Right to know → disclosure → citizen participation → social verification → grievance/appeal → corrective action → accountable service delivery.
