APPSC deep core

  • Good governance: rule of law, transparency, accountability, participation, responsiveness, equity, effectiveness and institutional capacity.
  • E-governance: digital technology can improve access, speed, traceability and integration, but digital exclusion, privacy, cybersecurity, data quality and interoperability matter.
  • Citizen's Charter: public commitment on service standards, timelines and grievance channels; effectiveness depends on measurable standards and enforcement/monitoring.
  • Do not equate digitisation alone with administrative reform.

Mains framework

Citizen need → service standard → process redesign → digital enablement → inclusion → transparency → grievance redress → performance measurement.
