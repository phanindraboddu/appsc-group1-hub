APPSC deep core

  • Indian federalism combines constitutionally divided powers with institutions and provisions that give the Union significant coordinating capacity.
  • Legislative relations: Article 246 and the Seventh Schedule distribute subjects through Union, State and Concurrent Lists; residuary legislative power rests with Parliament.
  • Administrative relations: revise Union–State coordination, inter-State mechanisms and All India Services in constitutional context.
  • Financial relations: taxation assignments, grants and the Finance Commission are core; connect contemporary fiscal federalism to the constitutional framework without assuming all revenue-sharing questions are fixed permanently.
  • Emergency provisions can alter the normal federal balance under constitutionally specified conditions.
  • Devolution: distinguish Union→State fiscal/administrative relations from State→local-body decentralisation under Parts IX and IXA.

Prelims focus

Seventh Schedule • three Lists • residuary power • Finance Commission • Inter-State Council • emergency effects • local devolution.

Mains framework

Federal design → legislative relations → administrative coordination → fiscal relations → dispute-resolution institutions → decentralisation → cooperative/competitive dynamics.
