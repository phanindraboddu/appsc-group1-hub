APPSC deep core

  • The Preamble identifies the source of authority as “We, the People of India” and states the constitutional objectives and character of the Republic.
  • Remember the sequence: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic; and Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
  • The 42nd Amendment added Socialist, Secular and integrity.
  • The Supreme Court's constitutional jurisprudence treats the Preamble as part of the Constitution and as an interpretive aid; it does not itself confer independent substantive powers.
  • For Mains, connect justice/liberty/equality/fraternity with Fundamental Rights, DPSPs and constitutional democracy.

Prelims traps

Preamble amended once • 42nd Amendment additions • “Republic” concerns an elected/non-hereditary head of state • distinguish constitutional ideals from enforceable rights.

Mains framework

Source of authority → nature of State → objectives → interpretive significance → relationship with rights/DPSPs → basic constitutional values.
