Directive Principles

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

Part IV; socialistic, Gandhian and liberal-intellectual principles; relationship with Fundamental Rights; welfare-state objectives and implementation.

Prelims checklist

  • Relevant Parts, Articles, Schedules and constitutional terminology
  • Composition, appointment/election, tenure and removal where applicable
  • Powers, functions, limitations and institutional relationships
  • Constitutional vs statutory basis
  • Andhra Pradesh application and current institutional context where relevant

Mains dimensions

Constitutional basis → purpose → institutional design → working → accountability → issues/challenges → reforms → AP relevance → balanced conclusion.

APPSC deep core

  • DPSPs are in Part IV, Articles 36–51. Article 37 makes them non-enforceable by courts while declaring them fundamental in governance.
  • Study themes rather than only labels: welfare/social order, distributive justice, equal pay/legal aid, village panchayats, work/education/public assistance, humane labour conditions, environment, monuments, separation of judiciary/executive and international peace.
  • Articles 39(b) and 39(c) are especially important in debates over distribution of material resources and concentration of wealth.
  • FR–DPSP jurisprudence developed through constitutional amendments and Supreme Court decisions; the constitutional design seeks harmony rather than automatic supremacy of one entire Part over the other.

Prelims focus

Article–principle matching • Part IV • Article 37 • Gandhian/social-economic/liberal-intellectual classification as a study aid, not constitutional headings.

Mains framework

Welfare-state purpose → non-justiciability → major principles → FR relationship → legislation/public policy → judicial interpretation → continuing relevance.
