APPSC deep core

  • Attitude: a learned evaluative orientation toward an object/person/issue; commonly analysed through cognitive, affective and behavioural components.
  • Attitudes may change through information, experience, persuasion, social influence and institutional context.
  • Emotional intelligence: broadly involves recognising, understanding and regulating one's emotions and responding effectively to others' emotions.
  • Administrative uses include conflict management, listening, teamwork, negotiation, stress regulation and citizen interaction; EI does not replace legality, competence or integrity.

Case-study use

Recognise emotion → avoid impulsive response → listen → identify interests → communicate respectfully → decide objectively → follow up.
