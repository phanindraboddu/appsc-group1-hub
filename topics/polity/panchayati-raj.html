APPSC deep core

  • 73rd Amendment: constitutionalised Panchayats through Part IX. Article 243B provides village, intermediate and district levels, subject to the constitutional population exception for the intermediate tier.
  • Gram Sabha: Article 243A; distinguish it from the elected Gram Panchayat.
  • Revise reservation, five-year duration, elections, State Election Commission, State Finance Commission and Eleventh Schedule subjects.
  • 74th Amendment: Part IXA. Article 243Q provides Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council and Municipal Corporation for different categories of urban areas.
  • Revise wards, reservations, duration, municipal finance/planning and Twelfth Schedule subjects.
  • APPSC angle: focus on the constitutional framework first, then Andhra Pradesh's statutory/local administrative structure as a separate layer.

Prelims traps

73rd vs 74th • Part IX vs IXA • 11th vs 12th Schedule • Gram Sabha vs Panchayat • SEC vs ECI • SFC vs Union Finance Commission.

Mains framework

Democratic decentralisation → constitutionalisation → functions → funds → functionaries → participation/social justice → planning → implementation gaps.
