APPSC deep core

Civil law generally concerns rights, obligations and civil remedies; criminal law concerns offences prosecuted by the State and prescribed punishments. Core criminal-justice concepts include legality, fair procedure, investigation, trial, evidence and appeal. For APPSC, prioritise syllabus concepts and verify current statutory provisions before memorising section numbers.

Prelims comparison

Civil vs criminal • substantive vs procedural law • investigation vs trial • remedy vs punishment.
