APPSC deep core

  • Public administration concerns organisation and management of public institutions and implementation of public policy.
  • Trace broad movement from traditional rule-bound administration toward development administration, New Public Management and contemporary governance/network approaches.
  • Core concepts: hierarchy, span of control, unity of command, delegation, coordination, accountability, efficiency, effectiveness and citizen orientation.
  • Distinguish government from broader governance, which can involve state, market and civil-society actors.

Mains framework

Meaning → evolution → changing State role → accountability → citizen-centricity → technology/collaboration → Indian administrative context.
