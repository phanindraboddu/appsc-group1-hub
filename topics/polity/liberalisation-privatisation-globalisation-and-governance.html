APPSC deep core

  • Liberalisation generally refers to reducing/reshaping state controls and barriers; privatisation to greater private ownership/participation; globalisation to deeper cross-border economic and institutional integration.
  • Post-1991 governance increasingly emphasised regulation, competition, contracting, public-private interaction and independent/specialised institutions alongside traditional departmental administration.
  • The State's role did not simply disappear: it shifted in many sectors toward rule-setting, regulation, social protection, market oversight and outcome monitoring.
  • Analyse effects through efficiency/innovation, fiscal implications, access/affordability, labour and regional distribution, consumer protection and regulatory capacity.
  • For APPSC, connect economic reform to governance transformation, not merely macroeconomic policy.

Prelims focus
