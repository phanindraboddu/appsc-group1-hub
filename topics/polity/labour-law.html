Core coverage

Constitutional labour protections, wages, industrial relations, social security and occupational conditions.

Definition/legal basis • institutions • powers/functions • accountability • issues • reforms • Andhra Pradesh relevance where applicable.

Mains practice

Explain the significance of Labour Law. Analyse major challenges and suggest institutionally appropriate improvements.

Rapid revision

Basis → 3 features → 2 issues → 2 reforms → 1 example.

APPSC deep core

  • Labour law balances worker protection, industrial relations, social security, occupational safety and conditions of employment with economic activity and enterprise compliance.
  • Prepare concepts such as wages, industrial disputes, collective bargaining, social security, workplace safety and protections for vulnerable workers.
  • India has enacted four labour codes consolidating numerous central labour laws: the Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Code on Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code. For examination questions, verify their latest commencement/rule status before stating that a particular provision is operational.
  • Distinguish constitutional labour principles, legislation, delegated rules and administrative enforcement.

Mains framework

Worker dignity/security → productive employment → industrial harmony → compliance/enforcement → informal-sector challenge → balanced labour governance.
