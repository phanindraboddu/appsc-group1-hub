APPSC deep core

  • Study Indian federalism through legislative, administrative and financial relations rather than labels alone.
  • The Governor is the constitutional head of the State; the parliamentary aid-and-advice framework is central, with discretion only where constitutionally permitted.
  • High-yield areas: appointment/term, executive and legislative functions, ordinance power, reservation of Bills, constitutional discretion and the Governor's place in Union–State relations.
  • For contemporary disputes, distinguish constitutional text, Supreme Court interpretation and political claims.

Mains framework

Federal design → Governor's constitutional position → aid/advice → limited discretion → Centre-State interface → judicial safeguards → cooperative federalism.
