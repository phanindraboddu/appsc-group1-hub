73rd & 74th Amendments

APPSC Group-I Paper III • Polity, Governance, Law & Ethics

Core coverage

Constitutionalisation of rural and urban local government; Gram Sabha, Panchayats, Municipalities, reservations, Finance/Election Commissions and Eleventh/Twelfth Schedules.

Prelims / factual checklist

  • Constitutional or statutory basis and key terminology
  • Institutional structure, powers, functions and safeguards
  • Important distinctions and common statement-question traps
  • Andhra Pradesh governance linkage where relevant

Mains framework

Definition/basis → rationale → institutional design → functioning → issues → accountability/ethical dimension → reforms → AP example → conclusion.

Practice checks

  1. Identify the legal/constitutional basis before evaluating an institution. Correct.
  2. Governance answers should connect principles with implementation. Correct.
  3. Unverified questions may be called official PYQs. Incorrect.

Practice only — not official PYQs.

APPSC deep core

73rd Amendment: Part IX, Panchayats, Gram Sabha, reservations, five-year term, State Election Commission, State Finance Commission and Eleventh Schedule. 74th Amendment: Part IXA, urban local bodies, reservations, elections/finance/planning and Twelfth Schedule.

Prelims traps

Part IX vs IXA • 11th vs 12th Schedule • Gram Sabha vs Gram Panchayat • SEC vs ECI • SFC vs Finance Commission.

Mains framework

Democratic decentralisation → functions → funds → functionaries → participation → planning → accountability → implementation gaps.
