APPSC deep core

  • Constitutional bodies derive their authority directly from the Constitution; statutory bodies from legislation; executive bodies from executive action.
  • Regulatory authorities oversee sectors or activities under powers granted by law; quasi-judicial authorities decide specified disputes or rights using adjudicatory procedures but are not ordinary courts.
  • Prepare every institution through: legal basis → composition → appointment → tenure/removal → jurisdiction → powers → reporting/accountability.
  • For tribunals, revise specialisation, access, independence and the continuing role of constitutional judicial review.

Prelims traps

Constitutional vs statutory • regulator vs tribunal • advisory vs adjudicatory • appointing authority vs reporting authority.

Mains framework

Need → legal basis → mandate → independence → accountability → expertise/access → judicial review → institutional improvement.
