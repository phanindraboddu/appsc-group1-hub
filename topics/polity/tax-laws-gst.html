Rapid revision

Basis → 3 features → 2 issues → 2 reforms → 1 example.

APPSC deep core

  • Start with the constitutional distinction between direct and indirect taxation, legislative competence and fiscal federalism.
  • GST: destination-based indirect tax framework introduced through the 101st Constitutional Amendment; understand CGST, SGST/UTGST and IGST architecture.
  • High-yield concepts: supply, taxable person/registration, input tax credit, tax invoice, returns, reverse charge, composition levy, zero-rated supply, exempt supply, e-way bill and adjudication/appeal at a conceptual level.
  • GST Council: constitutional body under Article 279A. Distinguish it from the Finance Commission under Article 280.
  • For rates, thresholds, procedural deadlines and recent amendments, always verify current notifications/statutory provisions because these can change.

Prelims traps

CGST+SGST vs IGST • zero-rated vs exempt • ITC vs refund • GST Council vs Finance Commission • direct vs indirect tax.

Mains framework

Pre-GST fragmentation → constitutional reform → dual GST design → common market/compliance benefits → federal/administrative challenges → technology → taxpayer facilitation.
