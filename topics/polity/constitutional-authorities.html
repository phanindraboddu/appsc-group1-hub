Constitutional Authorities

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

Election Commission, CAG, UPSC/SPSC, Finance Commission, Attorney General/Advocate General and other constitutionally established offices: appointment, tenure, powers and independence.

Prelims checklist

  • Relevant Parts, Articles, Schedules and constitutional terminology
  • Composition, appointment/election, tenure and removal where applicable
  • Powers, functions, limitations and institutional relationships
  • Constitutional vs statutory basis
  • Andhra Pradesh application and current institutional context where relevant

Mains dimensions

Constitutional basis → purpose → institutional design → working → accountability → issues/challenges → reforms → AP relevance → balanced conclusion.

Practice MCQs

  1. For Polity, the constitutional/legal basis should be identified before analysing an institution. Answer: True
  2. Constitutional and statutory bodies mean the same thing. Answer: False
  3. APPSC Mains answers benefit from linking constitutional principle with practical governance. Answer: True

APPSC deep core

  • Election Commission: Article 324 framework; study superintendence, direction and control of elections together with election laws.
  • Finance Commission: Article 280; constituted periodically to make recommendations on specified Union–State financial matters.
  • CAG: Articles 148–151; constitutional audit institution. Distinguish constitutional independence safeguards, duties/powers under law, audit reporting and legislative financial accountability.
  • UPSC/State PSCs: Articles 315 onward; recruitment/advisory functions and independence safeguards.
  • Attorney-General/Advocate-General: constitutional law officers of Union/State; compare appointment, qualifications and functions.
  • Also revise constitutional commissions/bodies for specified communities where the syllabus groups them under constitutional authorities.

Prelims matrix

Body → Article → appointing authority → tenure/removal safeguards → report recipient → core function.

Mains framework

Constitutional basis → independence → mandate → accountability/reporting → institutional constraints → role in democratic checks and balances.
