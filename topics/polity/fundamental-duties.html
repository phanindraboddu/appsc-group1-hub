APPSC deep core

  • Fundamental Duties are in Article 51A, Part IVA.
  • They were introduced by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment, 1976, influenced by the Swaran Singh Committee recommendations.
  • Originally ten duties were inserted; the 86th Amendment added the duty of a parent/guardian to provide opportunities for education to a child/ward aged 6–14.
  • High-yield duties include respecting the Constitution/national symbols, promoting harmony, protecting environment, developing scientific temper, safeguarding public property and striving for excellence.
  • Duties are not directly enforceable in the same manner as Fundamental Rights, but can inform legislation and constitutional interpretation.

Prelims traps

Part IVA • Article 51A • 42nd Amendment • originally 10, now 11 • education duty added by 86th Amendment.
