Revolt of 1857

APPSC Group-I • Detailed Prelims + Mains chapter

Core syllabus map

  • Background: annexations, military grievances and political-economic pressures
  • Meerut, Delhi and spread of the revolt
  • Major centres and leaders
  • Nature and limits of participation
  • British suppression and consequences
  • Historiographical interpretations

High-yield facts

  • The revolt began at Meerut in May 1857 and rapidly reached Delhi, where Bahadur Shah II became a symbolic figurehead.
  • Important centres included Kanpur, Lucknow, Jhansi, Bareilly and Bihar, with different local leaderships and motivations.
  • The revolt involved sepoys as well as sections of rulers, landed groups and civilians, but participation was uneven across regions.
  • The Government of India Act 1858 transferred rule from the East India Company to the British Crown.
  • After 1857 the British reorganised the army and altered policies toward princes and landed interests.

How to write the topic

Use background → actors → events/process → regional participation → consequences → historical significance. For Mains, distinguish documented events from later interpretations and avoid reducing a complex movement to one leader.

Prelims traps

  • Check year, leader and location together.
  • Distinguish a movement from the organisation or leader associated with it.
  • For Andhra questions, connect the regional event with its national context.
  • Do not label unverified questions as PYQs.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  2. A single leader explains the entire freedom movement. False
  3. Chronology is useful for identifying cause-and-effect relationships. True
