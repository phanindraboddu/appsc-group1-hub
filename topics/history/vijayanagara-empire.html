Vijayanagara Empire

APPSC Group-I • South India + AP • Deep reading chapter

Foundation & rulers

Vijayanagara was founded in the fourteenth century; Harihara and Bukka are traditionally associated with its foundation. Krishnadevaraya is the best-known ruler.

Political structure

The empire used royal authority, provincial arrangements and military elites. The nayaka system is important but should not be treated as identical everywhere or in every period.

Economy

Irrigation, agriculture, temple-centred economies and long-distance trade supported the empire. Horse trade and coastal ports were strategically important.

Krishnadevaraya

His reign is associated with military expansion, administrative activity, literature and temple patronage. Amuktamalyada is a major Telugu literary association.

Hampi

Hampi/Vijayanagara provides evidence through temples, markets, fortifications, water systems and urban remains.

Deccan politics

Vijayanagara competed with Bahmani and successor states over the Raichur Doab and wider Deccan.

APPSC angle

Study Telugu literary patronage, Andhra regional connections and the interaction with Gajapatis and Qutb Shahis.

Mains

Explain Vijayanagara through state formation + agrarian base + trade + military organisation + culture.
