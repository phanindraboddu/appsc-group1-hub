Andhra State 1953 & Andhra Pradesh 1956

High-yield facts

  • Potti Sriramulu's fast and death in 1952 became a major catalyst for the creation of Andhra State.
  • Andhra State was formed on 1 October 1953 from Telugu-speaking areas separated from the Madras State; Kurnool was the capital.
  • The States Reorganisation Commission was appointed in 1953.
  • Andhra Pradesh was formed on 1 November 1956 through reorganisation that brought Andhra State and the Telugu-speaking Telangana region of Hyderabad State together.
  • The episode is important for understanding linguistic federalism and state formation in independent India.

Prelims traps

  • Match dates, personalities, dynasties and institutions carefully.
  • Separate historical fact from later interpretation.
  • For reorganisation topics, distinguish statutory provisions from later implementation.
  • Use standard APPSC terminology for literary traditions.
