APPSC high-yield core

  • Andhra demand: the movement for a separate Andhra province developed over decades around linguistic identity, representation and administrative concerns.
  • Potti Sriramulu: his fast and death in December 1952 became the immediate catalyst for the Union government's decision to create Andhra State.
  • 1953: Andhra State came into existence on 1 October 1953, formed from Telugu-speaking districts of the then Madras State; Kurnool served as its capital.
  • Reorganisation debate: the States Reorganisation Commission considered the future of Telangana and Andhra in the wider linguistic reorganisation process.
  • 1956: Andhra Pradesh was formed on 1 November 1956 by merging Andhra State with the Telugu-speaking Telangana region of Hyderabad State.
  • Exam caution: distinguish clearly among Andhra movement, Andhra State (1953), States Reorganisation Commission and Andhra Pradesh (1956).

Prelims timeline

Long Andhra movement → Potti Sriramulu's fast/death (1952) → Andhra State (1 Oct 1953) → SRC process → Andhra Pradesh (1 Nov 1956).

Mains angle

Trace linguistic consciousness, political mobilisation, Potti Sriramulu, creation of Andhra State, SRC recommendations/debate and formation of Andhra Pradesh in the broader context of Indian federalism.
