Kakatiyas, Reddis, Gajapatis & Vijayanagara

APPSC Group-I • AP-specific integrated History chapter

Core map

  • Kakatiya Warangal and irrigation
  • Reddy kingdoms and Kondaveedu
  • Gajapati influence in coastal Andhra
  • Vijayanagara expansion
  • Krishnadevaraya and Andhra regions
  • Trade, temples and political transitions

High-yield facts

  • Kakatiya rule is closely associated with Warangal and tank irrigation.
  • Reddy kingdoms became important regional powers after Kakatiya decline.
  • Gajapati rulers of Odisha exercised influence in parts of coastal Andhra at different periods.
  • Vijayanagara became a major South Indian imperial power and Krishnadevaraya's reign is especially important for AP history.

Prelims traps

  • Match dynasty with region, inscription and period.
  • Do not treat literary traditions as identical to contemporary administrative records.
  • Keep political influence distinct from direct territorial control.
  • Use standard APPSC chronology where dates vary across sources.

Mains framework

Political transition → regional economy → irrigation/trade → language/literature → architecture/culture → wider Indian context → conclusion.
