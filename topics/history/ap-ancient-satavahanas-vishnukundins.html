AP Ancient: Satavahanas to Vishnukundins

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains • Andhra-focused where applicable

Core coverage

Ancient Andhra political and cultural history from Satavahana age through Ikshvakus and Vishnukundins; religion, economy, art, inscriptions and regional centres.

Prelims focus

  • Chronology, dynasties/leaders and locations
  • Inscriptions, texts, monuments and archaeological evidence
  • Administration, economy and social institutions
  • Religion, literature, art and architecture
  • Andhra-specific personalities, centres and developments

Mains dimensions

Context → chronology → political/administrative change → socio-economic conditions → religion/culture → literature/art → Andhra significance → critical conclusion.
