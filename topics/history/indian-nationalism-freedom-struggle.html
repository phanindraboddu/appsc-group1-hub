Indian Nationalism & Freedom Struggle — Integrated Revision

APPSC Group-I • Integrated revision chapter

Core map

  • Rise of nationalism and Congress
  • Moderates and extremists
  • Swadeshi and Home Rule
  • Gandhian mass politics
  • Revolutionary and socialist currents
  • Peasant, worker, tribal and women's participation
  • Constitutional negotiations and Partition

High-yield facts

  • Indian nationalism developed through multiple organisations, ideologies and social groups.
  • The Gandhian phase transformed the scale and methods of mass mobilisation but did not eliminate other political currents.
  • The freedom struggle included constitutional politics, mass movements, revolutionary activity and social reform.
  • Partition resulted from a complex political process involving multiple actors and negotiations.

Comparative method

For these synthesis topics, build answers around chronology + causation + comparison + regional variation + consequences. Link political developments with economy, society, religion, literature and material culture.

Prelims traps

  • Do not confuse dynasties occupying the same region at different times.
  • Match institutions and policies with the correct ruler/period.
  • Separate primary evidence from later literary tradition.
  • Use standard chronology where dates are debated.
