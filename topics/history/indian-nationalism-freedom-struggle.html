APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

Growth of nationalism; associations and INC; moderates/extremists; Swadeshi, Home Rule, Gandhian mass movements, revolutionary/constitutional strands, participation of different social groups and Andhra contributions.

Rapid revision

Definition/context → 5 key points → 3 examples → 2 challenges → 2 reforms → one-line conclusion.

Integrated revision core

  • Foundations: colonial economic critique, modern education, press, associations and political unification contributed to organised nationalism.
  • Moderates: constitutional agitation and economic critique; Swadeshi: boycott, national education and broader mobilisation after Bengal partition.
  • Home Rule: Tilak and Annie Besant; connect to the Lucknow phase and wartime politics.
  • Gandhian era: Rowlatt/Jallianwala → Non-Cooperation → Civil Disobedience → Quit India, with constructive work and negotiations alongside mass protest.
  • Diverse streams: revolutionaries, peasants/workers, socialists, constitutional politics, Subhas Bose/INA and anti-caste/social-justice movements complicate any single narrative.
  • Andhra: Chirala-Perala, Rampa/Manyam rebellion, Andhra leaders and local participation must be integrated into national chronology.

Prelims master chronology

INC → Swadeshi → Surat → Home Rule/Lucknow → Rowlatt/Jallianwala → NCM → Simon/Lahore → CDM → constitutional negotiations → Quit India → INA → transfer of power.

Mains synthesis

Origins → ideological phases → methods → mass participation → regional/social diversity → constitutional negotiations → wartime developments → independence and Partition.
