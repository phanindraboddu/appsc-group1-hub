Satavahanas, Sangam, Sungas & Guptas — Synthesis

APPSC Group-I • Integrated Prelims + Mains chapter

Core map

  • Post-Mauryan regionalisation
  • Satavahana Deccan
  • Sangam Tamilakam
  • Sunga period
  • Gupta consolidation
  • Trade, coinage, religion and literature

High-yield facts

  • Post-Mauryan India saw multiple regional states.
  • Satavahanas link Deccan trade, inscriptions and Buddhist/Brahmanical patronage.
  • Sangam sources illuminate early Tamil society and trade.
  • Gupta achievements should be balanced with social and regional complexity.

How to analyse

Use chronology → causation → political institutions → economy → society/religion → art/literature → regional variation → consequences.

Prelims traps

  • Match rulers, institutions and sources with the correct period.
  • Do not confuse later literary traditions with contemporary evidence.
  • Regional variation matters.
  • Use standard exam chronology when dates are debated.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  1. Historical change usually has multiple causes. True
  2. Political decline automatically means cultural decline. False
  3. Regional variation is important in Indian history. True
  4. These are official PYQs. False

Mains practice

Discuss the political, economic and cultural changes represented by Satavahanas, Sangam, Sungas & Guptas — Synthesis.

Structure: context → chronology → 3 themes → regional example → continuity/change → conclusion.
