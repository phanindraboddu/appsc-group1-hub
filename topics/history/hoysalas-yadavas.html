Hoysalas & Yadavas

APPSC Group-I • South & Deccan History • Deep reading chapter

Regional powers

Hoysalas emerged in Karnataka with centres including Dwarasamudra; Yadavas/Seunas became important in the western Deccan with Devagiri as a major centre.

Economy & state

Agrarian expansion, irrigation, temples and regional trade supported these kingdoms.

Architecture

Hoysala temples at Belur and Halebidu are known for detailed sculpture and distinctive architectural planning.

Deccan interaction

These states interacted and competed with Cholas, Pandyas, Kakatiyas and later Delhi Sultanate expansion.

APPSC Prelims

Dwarasamudra, Devagiri, Hoysala architecture, Deccan political map.

Mains angle

Explain the role of regional kingdoms in shaping the political and cultural landscape of medieval South India.
