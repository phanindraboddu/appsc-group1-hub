APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated study chapter

Core coverage

Prelims high-yield checklist

  • Key rulers/leaders and places
  • Texts, inscriptions and primary sources
  • Administration and institutions
  • Economy, society and religion
  • Art, architecture and literature
  • Andhra/Deccan connections where relevant

Mains answer framework

Introduction → historical context → major developments → political/administrative dimension → socio-economic dimension → culture/ideas → significance/limitations → Andhra relevance → conclusion.
