Eastern Chalukyas

APPSC Group-I • Andhra Regional History • Deep reading chapter

Vengi polity

Eastern Chalukyas ruled the Vengi region and became central to Andhra political history. Their position between Deccan and eastern-coast powers made alliances and conflicts important.

Chalukya-Chola interaction

Marriage alliances and political competition linked Vengi with the Cholas. Rajaraja Narendra is especially important for Telugu literary history.

Language & literature

The Eastern Chalukya period is associated with the rise of Telugu literary culture; Nannaya's Mahabharata translation is a core APPSC topic.

Sources & architecture

Inscriptions, temple remains and literary traditions provide evidence. Study inscriptions as evidence for land, administration and patronage.

APPSC Prelims

Vengi, Rajaraja Narendra, Nannaya, Chalukya-Chola relations.

Mains angle

Discuss how political interaction in Vengi contributed to the development of Telugu language and literature.
