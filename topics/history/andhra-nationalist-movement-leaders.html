Andhra Nationalist Movement & Leaders

APPSC Group-I • Andhra-focused Prelims + Mains

Core coverage

Andhra participation in the national movement: Swadeshi, Home Rule, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India and regional leaders, organisations and mass participation.

Prelims focus

  • Chronology and sequence
  • Places, dynasties/leaders and institutions
  • Inscriptions, literary works and authors
  • Religion, art and architecture
  • Andhra-specific political, social and economic developments

Mains dimensions

Background → chronology → political/administrative developments → society & economy → culture/literature → Andhra significance → continuity/change → conclusion.

Mains practice

Explain the major developments associated with Andhra Nationalist Movement & Leaders and assess their significance in the historical and cultural evolution of Andhra.

60-second revision method

Timeline → 5 names/places → 3 contributions → 2 sources → 1 Andhra-specific conclusion.

PYQ discipline

Exact APPSC/UPSC questions are added as PYQs only after verification against official papers.

APPSC high-yield core

  • Regional awakening: associations, newspapers, libraries, education and Telugu public culture helped expand political consciousness in coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema.
  • National movements: Andhra participation must be linked separately to Swadeshi, Home Rule, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience and Quit India.
  • Alluri Sitarama Raju: the Rampa/Manyam rebellion of the 1920s belongs to the tribal resistance context; study local grievances, colonial forest restrictions and its relationship to the wider nationalist atmosphere.
  • Andhra leaders: prepare figures such as Konda Venkatappayya, Tanguturi Prakasam, Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya and other syllabus/PYQ-relevant leaders with their specific movements and organisations.
  • Chirala-Perala: an important Andhra episode of anti-colonial mobilisation associated with municipal taxation and Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya.
  • Constructive work: khadi, national education, libraries, social reform and village-level organisation broadened nationalist politics beyond protest events.

Prelims focus

Leader–movement • place–agitation • newspaper/organisation • Rampa/Manyam chronology • Chirala-Perala • Andhra Congress and Home Rule associations.

Mains angle

Connect Andhra developments to all-India nationalism while showing distinct regional factors—tribal grievances, local taxation, Telugu public culture, social reform and regional leadership.
