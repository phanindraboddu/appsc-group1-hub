High-yield facts

  • Kavitrayam refers to Nannaya, Tikkana and Errana in the Telugu Mahabharata tradition.
  • Nannaya is traditionally associated with the court of Eastern Chalukya ruler Rajaraja Narendra.
  • Pothana is famous for the Telugu Bhagavatam tradition.
  • Krishnadevaraya authored Amuktamalyada in Telugu.
  • The Ashtadiggajas are traditionally associated with the literary court of Krishnadevaraya; exact membership lists should follow standard APPSC textbook convention.

Prelims traps

  • Match dates, personalities, dynasties and institutions carefully.
  • Separate historical fact from later interpretation.
  • For reorganisation topics, distinguish statutory provisions from later implementation.
  • Use standard APPSC terminology for literary traditions.

Mains framework

Context → historical development → key personalities/institutions → cultural/social/economic significance → regional and national link → balanced conclusion.
