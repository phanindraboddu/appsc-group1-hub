High-yield facts

  • The integration of princely states was a major early task of the Union government.
  • The States Reorganisation Commission was appointed in 1953 and its report influenced the 1956 reorganisation.
  • Andhra State was formed in 1953, an important landmark in linguistic state formation.
  • The States Reorganisation Act, 1956 reorganised major parts of the Indian Union and Andhra Pradesh was formed on 1 November 1956.
  • Post-independence consolidation involved not only territorial integration but institution building, economic development and management of linguistic and social diversity.

How to write the topic

Use background → actors → events/process → regional participation → consequences → historical significance. For Mains, distinguish documented events from later interpretations and avoid reducing a complex movement to one leader.

Prelims traps

  • Check year, leader and location together.
  • Distinguish a movement from the organisation or leader associated with it.
  • For Andhra questions, connect the regional event with its national context.
  • Do not label unverified questions as PYQs.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  1. Regional movements should be studied in both local and national context. True
  2. A single leader explains the entire freedom movement. False
  3. Chronology is useful for identifying cause-and-effect relationships. True
