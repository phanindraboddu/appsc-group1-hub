Pre-Historic & Ancient India — Integrated Revision

APPSC Group-I • Integrated Prelims + Mains chapter

Core map

  • Stone Age and early agriculture
  • Indus urbanism
  • Vedic and Mahajanapada transition
  • Jainism and Buddhism
  • Mauryas and Ashoka
  • Post-Mauryan contacts

High-yield facts

  • Prehistory relies mainly on archaeology; agriculture and settled life developed gradually.
  • Harappan urbanism is studied through planning, craft, trade and subsistence.
  • The sixth century BCE saw major political, economic and intellectual change.
  • Ashokan inscriptions are primary evidence for dhamma and public communication.

How to analyse

Use chronology → causation → political institutions → economy → society/religion → art/literature → regional variation → consequences. This is a synthesis chapter, so connect individual topics instead of memorising isolated rulers.

Prelims traps

  • Match rulers, institutions and sources with the correct period.
  • Do not confuse later literary traditions with contemporary evidence.
  • Regional variation matters.
  • Use standard exam chronology when dates are debated.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  1. Historical change usually has multiple causes. True
  2. Political decline automatically means cultural decline. False
  3. Regional variation is important in Indian history. True
  4. These are official PYQs. False
