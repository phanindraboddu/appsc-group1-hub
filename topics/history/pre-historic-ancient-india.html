High-yield facts

  • Prehistory is reconstructed mainly through archaeology; written sources become more important in later periods.
  • The transition to agriculture and settled life was gradual and regionally varied.
  • Harappan urbanism should be studied through planning, craft, trade, subsistence and regional diversity.
  • The sixth century BCE saw major political, economic and religious changes in the Ganga valley.
  • Mauryan inscriptions provide unusually direct evidence for Ashoka's policy and public communication.

Comparative method

For these synthesis topics, build answers around chronology + causation + comparison + regional variation + consequences. Link political developments with economy, society, religion, literature and material culture.

Prelims traps

  • Do not confuse dynasties occupying the same region at different times.
  • Match institutions and policies with the correct ruler/period.
  • Separate primary evidence from later literary tradition.
  • Use standard chronology where dates are debated.
