Pre-Historic & Ancient India

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated History

Core coverage

Prehistoric cultures and archaeological evidence; Indus civilisation; Vedic transition; Mahajanapadas and state formation; major social, economic, religious and cultural developments.

Prelims high-yield

Chronology • rulers/leaders • places • texts/inscriptions • institutions • terminology • art/architecture • causes and consequences • Andhra links.

Mains framework

Background → chronology → political/administrative features → society/economy → religion/culture → significance → Andhra connection → balanced conclusion.

Practice

Prepare a timeline, five factual prelims cards and a 250-word answer explaining the historical significance of Pre-Historic & Ancient India. Practice only; do not label a question PYQ unless verified from the official paper.

Rapid revision

5 dates/events → 5 personalities → 5 terms → 3 cultural contributions → 2 Andhra links → 1 mains thesis.

Integrated revision core

  • Prehistory: distinguish Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic and Chalcolithic phases through tools, subsistence, settlement and technology.
  • Harappan civilisation: urban planning, craft production, trade, script, major sites and archaeological evidence.
  • Vedic transition: compare Early and Later Vedic polity, economy, society and religion; connect later developments to territorial states.
  • Mahajanapadas & Magadha: second urbanisation, state formation and the material/geographical advantages of Magadha.
  • Jainism & Buddhism: doctrines, organisations, councils, patronage and social context.
  • Mauryas: sources, administration, Ashoka, dhamma, inscriptions and art.
  • Post-Mauryan/early historic: Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushans, Satavahanas, Sangam polities, trade and cultural interaction.
  • Guptas: polity, economy, literature, science and art, while treating the “Golden Age” label critically.

Prelims master sequence

Stone Age → Harappan → Vedic → Mahajanapadas → Mauryas → post-Mauryan powers/Satavahanas → Guptas → early medieval transition.

Mains master framework

Sources → political institutions → economy/urbanisation → society → religion/philosophy → art/literature/science → regional/Andhra connections → continuity and change.
