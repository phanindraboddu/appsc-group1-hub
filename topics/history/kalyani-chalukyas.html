Kalyani Chalukyas

APPSC Group-I • Deccan History • Deep reading chapter

Rise

Tailapa II revived Chalukya power in the western Deccan in the late tenth century. Kalyani became the political centre.

Chola conflicts

The Chalukyas and Cholas competed over the Tungabhadra-Krishna region and Vengi, producing repeated military conflict.

Administration & economy

Land revenue, feudatories, irrigation and temple-centred local economies supported political power.

Architecture

Lakkundi and other sites illustrate the distinctive Deccan architectural phase often called the Western Chalukyan style.

APPSC Prelims

Tailapa II, Kalyani, Chalukya-Chola rivalry, Deccan architecture.

Mains angle

Compare Kalyani Chalukya political competition with Chola expansion and explain its effect on the eastern Deccan.
