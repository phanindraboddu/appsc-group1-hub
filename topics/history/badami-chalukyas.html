Badami Chalukyas

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

Pulakeshin II, Aihole inscription, Pallava rivalry, administration and architecture at Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal.

Prelims checklist

Chronology • personalities/dynasties • places • texts/inscriptions • administration • economy • religion • art/architecture • source-based distinctions.

Mains framework

Context → political developments → administration → society/economy → religion/culture → art/literature → historical significance → Andhra/Deccan linkage where relevant.

Practice only — not PYQs.

Mains practice

Discuss the political, socio-economic and cultural significance of Badami Chalukyas.

Revision rule

Remember 5 facts + 3 sources/places + 2 contributions + 1 analytical conclusion.

Exam-ready core

  • Political centre: Vatapi (Badami) was the capital; Aihole and Pattadakal are essential cultural sites.
  • Pulakeshin II: the best-known ruler; revise his conflict with Harsha and relations with Pallavas. The Aihole inscription of Ravikirti is a key source.
  • Deccan politics: Chalukya-Pallava rivalry shaped the peninsula and linked western Deccan politics with eastern/southern regions.
  • Administration: monarchy worked through territorial divisions, feudatories and local institutions; inscriptions are important evidence.
  • Architecture: Badami cave temples, Aihole experiments and Pattadakal monuments illustrate the development and interaction of northern and southern temple forms.

Prelims focus

Revise ruler–inscription, capital–site, Chalukya–Pallava conflicts and Badami–Aihole–Pattadakal monument associations.

Mains angle

Explain the Badami Chalukyas as both a major Deccan political power and an important bridge in the evolution of early medieval temple architecture.
