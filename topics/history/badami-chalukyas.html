Badami Chalukyas

APPSC Group-I • Deccan History • Deep reading chapter

Origins & chronology

Pulakeshin I consolidated Chalukya power at Vatapi/Badami. Pulakeshin II became the most important ruler, checked Harsha in the north and expanded across the Deccan.

Administration & polity

Study the relationship between the king, feudatories, military power and regional centres. The Aihole inscription of Ravikirti is a key source for Pulakeshin II.

Religion & society

Brahmanical traditions were prominent, while Jain and Buddhist traditions also existed. Do not reduce the period to one religious tradition.

Architecture

Badami caves, Aihole and Pattadakal are essential. Compare rock-cut and structural traditions and note the development of Deccan temple styles.

APPSC Prelims

Remember: Vatapi/Badami, Pulakeshin II, Aihole inscription, Pattadakal and the conflict with Harsha.

Mains angle

Explain how Chalukya power linked political expansion with Deccan cultural synthesis and temple architecture.

Prelims rapid revision

  • Chronology and ruler–event matching
  • Important inscription/text and what it tells us
  • Capital/centre, region and architectural site
  • Political conflict and neighbouring powers
  • Economy, irrigation, trade and cultural patronage
