High-yield facts

  • The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 provided the legal framework for the creation of Telangana from the erstwhile State of Andhra Pradesh.
  • Telangana came into existence on 2 June 2014.
  • The Act addressed representation, institutions, assets and liabilities, public services and other consequences of reorganisation.
  • The reorganisation had major implications for fiscal planning, infrastructure, institutions and regional development in the residuary Andhra Pradesh.
  • For examination answers, distinguish what the Act legally provided from later implementation developments and political claims.

Prelims traps

  • Match dates, personalities, dynasties and institutions carefully.
  • Separate historical fact from later interpretation.
  • For reorganisation topics, distinguish statutory provisions from later implementation.
  • Use standard APPSC terminology for literary traditions.
