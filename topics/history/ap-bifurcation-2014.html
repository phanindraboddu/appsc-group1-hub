AP Bifurcation 2014

APPSC Group-I • Andhra-focused Prelims + Mains

Core coverage

Historical background to bifurcation, Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act 2014, institutional and administrative division, capital/resource issues and post-bifurcation challenges; study legal provisions separately from political claims.

Prelims focus

  • Chronology and sequence
  • Places, dynasties/leaders and institutions
  • Inscriptions, literary works and authors
  • Religion, art and architecture
  • Andhra-specific political, social and economic developments

Mains dimensions

Background → chronology → political/administrative developments → society & economy → culture/literature → Andhra significance → continuity/change → conclusion.

PYQ discipline

APPSC high-yield core

  • The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Act 6 of 2014) provided the statutory framework for reorganisation of the existing State.
  • The Act received enactment on 1 March 2014; the notified appointed day was 2 June 2014, when Telangana and the residuary State of Andhra Pradesh came into existence.
  • Study the Act thematically: territories/boundaries; representation and legislatures; High Court/judicial arrangements; public services; assets/liabilities; corporations/institutions; water/resources; infrastructure and other transitional provisions.
  • Exam caution: distinguish provisions contained in the Act from later administrative decisions, court decisions, Union/State policy measures and political claims.
  • For Mains, analyse reorganisation through constitutional federalism, administrative transition and allocation issues without partisan evaluation.

Prelims focus

Act number/year • appointed day • territorial provisions • legislative representation • institutional/resource allocation terminology.

Mains framework

Background → parliamentary legislation → appointed day → territorial/institutional reorganisation → assets/liabilities/services/resources → administrative challenges → federal mechanisms for resolving outstanding issues.
