Mughals, Marathas & Europeans — Synthesis

APPSC Group-I • Integrated revision chapter

Core map

  • Mughal consolidation
  • Mansabdari, jagirdari and revenue
  • Aurangzeb and regionalisation
  • Maratha state and Chhatrapati Shivaji
  • European trading companies
  • Eighteenth-century political transition
  • Commerce to territorial power

High-yield facts

  • Mughal expansion created a large imperial framework but depended on negotiated relations with regional elites.
  • Shivaji's state used forts, revenue arrangements and military organisation suited to the western Deccan.
  • European companies initially operated as commercial organisations before acquiring political and territorial power in the eighteenth century.
  • The eighteenth century should be analysed through regional state formation alongside Mughal weakening, not as a simple political vacuum.

Comparative method

For these synthesis topics, build answers around chronology + causation + comparison + regional variation + consequences. Link political developments with economy, society, religion, literature and material culture.

Prelims traps

  • Do not confuse dynasties occupying the same region at different times.
  • Match institutions and policies with the correct ruler/period.
  • Separate primary evidence from later literary tradition.
  • Use standard chronology where dates are debated.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  2. Political decline automatically means complete cultural decline. False
