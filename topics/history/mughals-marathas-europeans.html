Mughals, Marathas & Europeans

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated History

Core coverage

Mughal expansion and administration, mansabdari/jagirdari, culture and economy; Maratha rise and institutions; European companies and changing coastal trade.

Prelims high-yield

Chronology • rulers/leaders • places • texts/inscriptions • institutions • terminology • art/architecture • causes and consequences • Andhra links.

Mains framework

Background → chronology → political/administrative features → society/economy → religion/culture → significance → Andhra connection → balanced conclusion.

Practice

Prepare a timeline, five factual prelims cards and a 250-word answer explaining the historical significance of Mughals, Marathas & Europeans. Practice only; do not label a question PYQ unless verified from the official paper.

Rapid revision

5 dates/events → 5 personalities → 5 terms → 3 cultural contributions → 2 Andhra links → 1 mains thesis.

Integrated revision core

  • Mughals: chronology from Babur to Aurangzeb; mansab-jagir system, revenue administration, imperial culture and Deccan expansion.
  • Marathas: Shivaji's state-building, forts, administration and later confederacy structure; distinguish Shivaji's institutions from later eighteenth-century developments.
  • Europeans: Portuguese, Dutch, English and French commercial networks developed through coastal factories and alliances.
  • Eighteenth century: Mughal political fragmentation, regional powers and European military-commercial competition interacted to transform the balance of power.
  • Andhra link: Coromandel trade, Machilipatnam, Northern Circars and Deccan succession politics connect this story directly to Andhra.

Prelims focus

Mughal ruler–policy • mansab/jagir • Maratha terminology • European company–settlement • Carnatic conflicts • Andhra ports.

Mains synthesis

Mughal imperial system → Deccan expansion → regional assertion → Maratha power → European commerce/rivalry → Company territorial power.
