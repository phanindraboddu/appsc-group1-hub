Core map

  • Pallavas and Chalukyas

  • Palas and Pratiharas
  • Cholas and later Chalukyas
  • Regional state formation
  • Temple institutions, agrarian expansion and literature

High-yield facts

  • Early medieval India is best studied through regional state formation and agrarian expansion.
  • The tripartite struggle for Kanauj involved Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas.
  • Chola inscriptions provide evidence on land, taxation, irrigation and local institutions.

How to analyse

Use chronology → causation → political institutions → economy → society/religion → art/literature → regional variation → consequences.

Prelims traps

  • Match rulers, institutions and sources with the correct period.
  • Do not confuse later literary traditions with contemporary evidence.
  • Regional variation matters.
  • Use standard exam chronology when dates are debated.
