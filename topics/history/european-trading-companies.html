European Trading Companies

APPSC Group-I • Modern India — Transition to Company Rule • Deep reading chapter

Portuguese

The Portuguese established an early European maritime presence in the Indian Ocean, with Goa becoming a major base. Study their naval strategy, cartaz system and spice trade.

Dutch, English & French

Dutch, English and French companies competed for Indian Ocean commerce. Their factories and commercial privileges gradually became linked with political intervention.

English East India Company

The English Company expanded from coastal trading centres to territorial power through wars, diplomacy, revenue rights and alliances.

Andhra connection

Masulipatnam was a major centre of European commercial activity and textile trade on the Andhra coast.

From commerce to rule

The eighteenth century is the key transition: commercial competition increasingly intersected with succession politics, military power and revenue collection.

Prelims

Revise company-country matching, important factories/ports and the chronology of major commercial-to-political transitions.

Mains

Explain why European companies could transform commercial privileges into political power in eighteenth-century India.
