European Trade & Company Rule in Andhra

APPSC Group-I • Detailed Prelims + Mains chapter

Core syllabus map

  • Masulipatnam/Machilipatnam as a major port

  • Transition from commerce to territorial rule

High-yield facts

  • Masulipatnam was an important port and textile-trading centre on the Andhra coast.
  • European companies competed for textiles, spices and other commodities and established factories and commercial privileges.
  • The political weakening of regional powers in the eighteenth century created opportunities for expanding Company influence.
  • The Northern Circars became important to the extension of British political and revenue control in coastal Andhra.
  • For APPSC, connect coastal commerce with the broader transition from European trade to colonial territorial power.

How to write the topic

Use background → actors → events/process → regional participation → consequences → historical significance. For Mains, distinguish documented events from later interpretations and avoid reducing a complex movement to one leader.

Prelims traps

  • Check year, leader and location together.
  • Distinguish a movement from the organisation or leader associated with it.
  • For Andhra questions, connect the regional event with its national context.
  • Do not label unverified questions as PYQs.
