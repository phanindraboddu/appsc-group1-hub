High-yield facts

  • Plassey (1757) and Buxar (1764) were major milestones in Company political expansion in Bengal.
  • The Company acquired Diwani rights in Bengal, Bihar and Orissa in 1765.
  • Revenue systems differed regionally; Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari and Mahalwari should not be treated as identical.
  • Subsidiary Alliance and Doctrine of Lapse were important instruments of expansion, but their operation varied across states.
  • Economic effects included changes in trade, taxation, handicrafts and agrarian relations.

Comparative method

For these synthesis topics, build answers around chronology + causation + comparison + regional variation + consequences. Link political developments with economy, society, religion, literature and material culture.

Prelims traps

  • Do not confuse dynasties occupying the same region at different times.
  • Match institutions and policies with the correct ruler/period.
  • Separate primary evidence from later literary tradition.
  • Use standard chronology where dates are debated.
