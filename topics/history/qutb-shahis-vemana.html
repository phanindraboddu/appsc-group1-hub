Qutb Shahis & Vemana

APPSC Group-I • AP-specific History & Culture • Detailed chapter

High-yield facts

  • The Qutb Shahi dynasty ruled the Golconda Sultanate from the sixteenth to seventeenth centuries.
  • Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah is associated with the foundation of Hyderabad in 1591 according to standard historical accounts.
  • Golconda was important in the diamond and textile trade networks of the Deccan.
  • Vemana's verses are noted for social and ethical criticism in accessible Telugu; his exact chronology is debated.
  • Qutb Shahi culture illustrates interaction among Telugu, Persian, Islamic and Deccani traditions.

Prelims traps

  • Match dates, personalities, dynasties and institutions carefully.
  • Separate historical fact from later interpretation.
  • For reorganisation topics, distinguish statutory provisions from later implementation.
  • Use standard APPSC terminology for literary traditions.

Mains framework

Context → historical development → key personalities/institutions → cultural/social/economic significance → regional and national link → balanced conclusion.
