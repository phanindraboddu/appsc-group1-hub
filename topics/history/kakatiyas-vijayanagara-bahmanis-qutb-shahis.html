Kakatiyas, Vijayanagara, Bahmanis & Qutb Shahis

APPSC Group-I • Integrated revision chapter

Core map

  • Kakatiya Warangal
  • Vijayanagara foundation and expansion
  • Bahmani Sultanate and Deccan politics
  • Nayaka institutions and military organisation
  • Irrigation, agriculture and trade
  • Golconda/Qutb Shahi rule
  • Architecture and composite Deccan culture

High-yield facts

  • Kakatiyas are important for Warangal, tank irrigation and regional Andhra-Telangana political history.
  • Vijayanagara became a major South Indian imperial power with a capital at Hampi/Vijayanagara.
  • The Bahmani Sultanate was a major Deccan power and later fragmented into successor states.
  • The Qutb Shahis ruled Golconda and contributed to the political and cultural history of the eastern Deccan.

Comparative method

For these synthesis topics, build answers around chronology + causation + comparison + regional variation + consequences. Link political developments with economy, society, religion, literature and material culture.

Prelims traps

  • Do not confuse dynasties occupying the same region at different times.
  • Match institutions and policies with the correct ruler/period.
  • Separate primary evidence from later literary tradition.
  • Use standard chronology where dates are debated.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs

  1. Historical change should be analysed through more than one cause. True
  2. Political decline automatically means complete cultural decline. False
  3. Regional variation is important in Indian history. True
  4. These questions are official PYQs. False

Mains practice

“Political change in Indian history was closely linked with economic, social and cultural change.” Discuss with reference to Kakatiyas, Vijayanagara, Bahmanis & Qutb Shahis.

Plan: context → chronology → 3 thematic dimensions → regional example → continuity/change → conclusion.
