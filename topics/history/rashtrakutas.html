Rashtrakutas

APPSC Group-I • Deccan & Early Medieval India • Deep reading chapter

Rise & expansion

Dantidurga established Rashtrakuta power after challenging the Chalukyas. Krishna I, Dhruva and Amoghavarsha I are important rulers.

Tripartite struggle

Rashtrakutas participated in the contest for Kanauj with the Palas and Gurjara-Pratiharas. This should be understood as repeated campaigns and competing claims rather than continuous direct control of Kanauj.

Administration & economy

The empire relied on regional elites, land revenue and agrarian expansion. Trade networks connected the Deccan with wider Indian Ocean commerce.

Culture

Amoghavarsha I is associated with literary patronage. The Kailasa temple at Ellora is traditionally associated with Krishna I.

APPSC Prelims

Dantidurga, Krishna I, Amoghavarsha I, Kailasa at Ellora, tripartite struggle.

Mains angle

Assess the Rashtrakutas as a bridge between Deccan political power and wider early-medieval cultural networks.

  • Economy, irrigation, trade and cultural patronage
