Sangam Age

APPSC Group-I • South India + APPSC Ancient History • Reading-ready chapter

1. Sources

Sangam literature is the major literary source, supplemented by archaeology, inscriptions, coins and foreign references. Literary texts should be read critically because compilation and dating are complex.

2. Three major political traditions

Chera, Chola and Pandya polities are central to Sangam political history. Political authority was often linked to chiefs, warfare, tribute and control of resources.

3. Society

Sangam texts describe diverse communities, occupations and social relationships. The concept of tinai links landscape with livelihood, emotions and cultural expression.

4. Economy and trade

Agriculture, pastoralism, craft production and maritime trade were important. Ports and commercial contacts connected Tamilakam with the wider Indian Ocean world.

5. Literature

Akam literature deals primarily with love and inner life; Puram focuses on public life, war, kingship and generosity. This distinction is useful for Prelims.

6. Religion and culture

Hero stones, local traditions and Brahmanical as well as other religious practices appear in the wider historical setting.

7. APPSC angle

Connect Sangam maritime trade with the early historic economy of the Deccan and Andhra coast. Do not collapse Tamilakam and Andhra political histories into one polity.

8. Mains

Use sources → polity → society → economy/trade → literature → cultural significance.
