Sangam Age

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

Chera, Chola and Pandya polities; Sangam literature, society, economy, ports, Roman trade and cultural life.

Prelims checklist

Chronology • personalities/dynasties • places • texts/inscriptions • administration • economy • religion • art/architecture • source-based distinctions.

Mains framework

Context → political developments → administration → society/economy → religion/culture → art/literature → historical significance → Andhra/Deccan linkage where relevant.

Practice only — not PYQs.

Mains practice

Discuss the political, socio-economic and cultural significance of Sangam Age.

Revision rule

Remember 5 facts + 3 sources/places + 2 contributions + 1 analytical conclusion.

Exam-ready core

  • Sources: Sangam literature, Tamil epics, inscriptions, coins and archaeology illuminate early historic south India.
  • Polities: Chera, Chola and Pandya rulers and smaller chiefs appear in the literary tradition; prepare their geographical zones and important ports.
  • Society: the tinai landscape framework links ecology with occupations, emotions and social life.
  • Economy: agriculture, pastoral activity, crafts, inland exchange and maritime trade were important; Roman-world contacts are evidenced by archaeology and coins.
  • Ports/trade: revise prominent west/east coast centres and commodities without treating literary descriptions as precise modern statistics.
  • Culture: heroic traditions, bardic poetry, religious diversity and evolving social differentiation are key themes.

Prelims focus

Prepare dynasty–ruler–capital/port associations, Sangam texts and authors where securely known, tinai terminology and archaeological evidence for overseas trade.

Mains dimensions

Use literature as historical evidence critically: political organisation, ecological zones, society, women, economy/trade, religion and the strengths/limits of literary sources.
