Eastern Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas & Renati Cholas

APPSC Group-I • AP-specific integrated History chapter

Core map

  • Vengi and Eastern Chalukyas
  • Rashtrakuta Deccan influence
  • Renati Cholas of Kadapa region
  • Early Telugu inscriptions and language
  • Trade, agriculture and temple culture
  • Regional political transitions

High-yield facts

  • Eastern Chalukyas were central to Vengi politics and later Telugu literary development.
  • Renati Chola inscriptions are important evidence for early Telugu usage in the Kadapa region.
  • Rashtrakuta power shaped the wider Deccan political environment.
  • Nannaya's literary tradition is linked to the Eastern Chalukya court of Rajaraja Narendra.

Prelims traps

  • Match dynasty with region, inscription and period.
  • Do not treat literary traditions as identical to contemporary administrative records.
  • Keep political influence distinct from direct territorial control.
  • Use standard APPSC chronology where dates vary across sources.

Mains framework

Political transition → regional economy → irrigation/trade → language/literature → architecture/culture → wider Indian context → conclusion.
