Delhi Sultanate

APPSC Group-I • Medieval India • Deep reading chapter

Dynasties & chronology

Mamluk/Slave, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid and Lodi dynasties form the standard sequence. Learn the major rulers and one defining policy/event for each.

Administration

Iqta was a revenue assignment used within the administrative and military system; it was not simply private ownership of land. Sultanate administration combined central officials, provincial arrangements and local intermediaries.

Alauddin Khalji

Market regulation, revenue measures and military reforms were connected with his attempt to sustain a large army and imperial expansion.

Muhammad bin Tughlaq

His reign is studied through experiments such as token currency, transfer of capital and ambitious campaigns; distinguish policy intention from outcomes.

Economy & society

Agriculture supported revenue; towns, crafts and trade expanded in several regions. Persianate institutions interacted with existing local structures.

Culture

Indo-Islamic architecture developed through interaction with Indian building traditions. Persian literature and regional languages also developed.

APPSC angle

Connect Delhi Sultanate expansion into the Deccan with Kakatiyas, Warangal and the later Bahmani state.

Mains

Assess the Sultanate as a process of political expansion, administrative experimentation and regional interaction.
