Delhi Sultanate, Bhakti & Sufi

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated History

Core coverage

Sultanate dynasties and institutions; iqta and administration; economy and architecture; Bhakti and Sufi traditions, major teachers and socio-cultural influence.

Prelims high-yield

Chronology • rulers/leaders • places • texts/inscriptions • institutions • terminology • art/architecture • causes and consequences • Andhra links.

Mains framework

Background → chronology → political/administrative features → society/economy → religion/culture → significance → Andhra connection → balanced conclusion.

Practice

Prepare a timeline, five factual prelims cards and a 250-word answer explaining the historical significance of Delhi Sultanate, Bhakti & Sufi. Practice only; do not label a question PYQ unless verified from the official paper.

Rapid revision

5 dates/events → 5 personalities → 5 terms → 3 cultural contributions → 2 Andhra links → 1 mains thesis.

Integrated revision core

  • Revise Sultanate dynasties in sequence: Mamluk/Slave, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid and Lodi.
  • Institutions: iqta, military organisation, revenue administration and court-centred sovereignty changed across dynasties.
  • Alauddin Khalji: market regulations, military/fiscal measures and Deccan campaigns are frequent exam areas.
  • Muhammad bin Tughlaq: study policy experiments with context and outcomes rather than caricature.
  • Bhakti: regionally diverse devotional traditions helped expand vernacular religious expression.
  • Sufism: silsilas, khanqahs and devotional practice; Chishti tradition is especially high-yield.
  • Andhra link: Deccan political change and vernacular devotional currents prepare the background for later Bahmani, Vijayanagara and Telugu cultural developments.

Prelims drill

Dynasty order • ruler–policy • iqta • saint–tradition • silsila • text/language • monument.

Mains synthesis

State formation → economy/administration → urban culture → religious interaction → vernacularisation → social reach and limitations.
