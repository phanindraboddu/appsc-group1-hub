Delhi Sultanate, Bhakti & Sufi — Synthesis

APPSC Group-I • Integrated Prelims + Mains chapter

Core map

  • Sultanate dynasties and administration
  • Iqta and revenue
  • Alauddin Khalji and Muhammad bin Tughlaq
  • Bhakti traditions
  • Sufi silsilas
  • Vernacular culture

High-yield facts

  • Sultanate control varied across regions and interacted with regional kingdoms.
  • Bhakti and Sufi traditions were diverse, not a single uniform movement.
  • Persian and vernacular cultures developed in overlapping political and social settings.

How to analyse

Use chronology → causation → political institutions → economy → society/religion → art/literature → regional variation → consequences.

Prelims traps

  • Match rulers, institutions and sources with the correct period.
  • Do not confuse later literary traditions with contemporary evidence.
  • Regional variation matters.
  • Use standard exam chronology when dates are debated.

Practice MCQs — not official PYQs
