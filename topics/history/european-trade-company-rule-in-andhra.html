European Trade & Company Rule in Andhra

APPSC Group-I • Prelims + Mains integrated

Core coverage

European factories and coastal trade; Northern Circars and Company expansion; revenue/administrative change, local economy and society, resistance and the transition to colonial rule in Andhra.

Prelims focus

Chronology • personalities • places • organisations • acts/events • Andhra links.

Mains framework

Context → key features → institutional/social/economic dimensions → issues → evidence/examples → reforms/way forward → conclusion.

Practice

Prepare five factual cards and one 250-word analytical answer on European Trade & Company Rule in Andhra. Practice only; official PYQ status requires verification from the APPSC paper.

Rapid revision

Definition/context → 5 key points → 3 examples → 2 challenges → 2 reforms → one-line conclusion.

APPSC high-yield core

  • The Andhra coast formed part of the wider Coromandel commercial system; textiles and ports attracted Portuguese, Dutch, English and French traders.
  • Machilipatnam/Masulipatnam: a key port for European commercial activity and Indian merchant networks.
  • European rivalry gradually became entangled with Indian political conflicts, especially during the eighteenth century.
  • Northern Circars: learn their political transfer and growing Company control as part of the expansion of British power in coastal Andhra.
  • Ceded Districts: connect the expansion of Company administration in Rayalaseema with the changing political map of south India.
  • For Mains, distinguish commercial penetration, political acquisition and later administrative/revenue consolidation.

European sequence • ports/factories • Machilipatnam • Northern Circars • Ceded Districts • Carnatic/Anglo-French context.

Mains framework

Coastal trade → factories → European rivalry → alliances/wars → territorial acquisition → revenue administration → effects on Andhra economy and society.
