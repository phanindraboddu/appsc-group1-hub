European Trade & Company Rule in Andhra

APPSC Group-I • AP-specific integrated History chapter

Core map

  • Masulipatnam and coastal commerce
  • Portuguese, Dutch, English and French companies
  • Textiles and maritime trade
  • Factories and political privileges
  • Northern Circars
  • Company territorial expansion

High-yield facts

  • Masulipatnam was a major port and textile centre on the Andhra coast.
  • European companies competed for commercial access and textiles before expanding political influence.
  • The Northern Circars became important to the expansion of British authority in coastal Andhra.
  • For Mains, connect coastal trade with the eighteenth-century transition from commerce to territorial revenue and administration.

Prelims traps

  • Match dynasty with region, inscription and period.
  • Do not treat literary traditions as identical to contemporary administrative records.
  • Keep political influence distinct from direct territorial control.
  • Use standard APPSC chronology where dates vary across sources.

Mains framework

Political transition → regional economy → irrigation/trade → language/literature → architecture/culture → wider Indian context → conclusion.
